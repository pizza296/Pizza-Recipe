PIZZA RECIPE — FREE iPHONE OFFLINE APP

WHAT IS INCLUDED
index.html, app.js, styles.css, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, and 3 icon PNGs.
All are needed. No paid service or personal information is required.

PUBLISH FOR FREE USING GITHUB PAGES
1. Create a free GitHub account at https://github.com if you do not have one.
   Choose a username that does not reveal your full name, keep your email private,
   and enable two-factor authentication.
2. Create a new PUBLIC repository, e.g. pizza-recipe.
3. Upload the 8 app files from the unzipped pizza-recipe-app folder into the
   repository's ROOT (not a nested folder). Commit the changes.
4. Go to repository Settings > Pages. Under Build and deployment, choose
   Deploy from a branch, main, /(root), then Save.
5. Wait for the Pages URL, normally https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/pizza-recipe/
   The exact URL is shown under Settings > Pages.
6. Open that HTTPS URL in Safari on your iPhone while online. Wait until the
   green 'Offline ready' status appears.
7. In Safari tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add. If offered 'Open as Web App',
   keep it enabled.
8. Open the new Pizza Recipe Home Screen icon WHILE ONLINE at least once and
   wait for 'Offline ready' INSIDE THAT INSTALLED APP. This step matters because
   Safari and Home Screen web apps can have separate storage.
9. TEST: Switch on Airplane Mode, turn Wi-Fi off, close and reopen the Home
   Screen app, and change the pizza count. Only rely on offline use after this
   test succeeds.

UPDATING THE RECIPE
Replace edited files in the SAME GitHub repository and keep the SAME Pages URL.
When you change app files, increase the CACHE value in sw.js from
pizza-recipe-v1 to pizza-recipe-v2 (and update the corresponding cache name
in app.js if changed). While online, open the app, use Check for recipe updates,
then close and reopen. Recheck 'Offline ready'.

PRIVACY AND COST
GitHub Pages is free for public repositories, but the app source and recipe
are public. Never upload passwords or private data. The app itself has no
analytics, advertising or third-party dependencies. Your chosen settings are
stored locally on your device. Hosting and access are free; internet provider
charges may apply while uploading or fetching updates.

NOTE
Opening index.html directly from Files will not enable service-worker offline
caching. You must host it over HTTPS (GitHub Pages is suitable).
